Design a simple MVP Payroll & Timesheets module for an AI Workforce Intelligence Platform. The goal is to help companies manage employee timesheets, calculate payroll-ready work hours, track billable hours, generate invoices, and export payroll data without building a full HRMS or accounting system.

### 1. Payroll & Timesheets Dashboard

Display Total Employees, Pending Timesheets, Approved Timesheets, Total Billable Hours, Total Work Hours, Payroll Summary, Upcoming Payroll Cycle, and Quick Actions.

### 2. Timesheets

Allow employees to view, create, edit, and submit daily, weekly, and monthly timesheets. Include work hours, billable hours, project allocation, approval status, and notes.

### 3. Timesheet Approvals

Allow managers to review, approve, reject, and comment on submitted timesheets. Show pending approvals, approval history, and employee summaries.

### 4. Billable Hours

Track billable and non-billable hours by employee, team, project, and client. Provide utilization summaries and billing breakdowns.

### 5. Payroll Calculations

Generate payroll-ready calculations based on approved work hours, overtime, hourly rates, salary structures, and attendance data. Display payroll summaries for employees and teams.

### 6. Payroll Reports

Provide payroll summaries, employee payroll reports, work-hour reports, overtime reports, billable-hour reports, and payroll history.

### 7. Invoice Support

Generate invoices based on billable hours, projects, clients, and approved timesheets. Allow invoice previews, invoice history, and export options.

### 8. Export Center

Allow exporting payroll reports, timesheets, billable hours, invoices, and payroll summaries in common formats.

### 9. Manager Workspace

Allow managers to review team timesheets, approve work hours, monitor billable utilization, review payroll summaries, and track team productivity.

### 10. Admin Workspace

Allow administrators to manage payroll settings, hourly rates, salary structures, approval workflows, payroll cycles, invoice settings, exports, and organization-wide payroll analytics.

### 11. Settings

Manage payroll rules, overtime rules, billing rates, invoice settings, approval workflows, notifications, permissions, and integrations.

Include dashboards, tables, payroll summaries, approval workflows, filters, search, exports, reports, notifications, role-based permissions, and responsive layouts. Focus on simplicity and payroll readiness rather than building a complete payroll or accounting platform.
