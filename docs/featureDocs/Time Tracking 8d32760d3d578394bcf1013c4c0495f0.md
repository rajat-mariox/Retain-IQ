Breadcrumb:

Dashboard > Time Tracking

Design a complete Time Tracking module for an AI Workforce Intelligence Platform. The module should help employees track time, managers monitor team productivity, and administrators manage workforce visibility. Create the following primary screens:

### 1. Time Tracking Dashboard

Display Today's Hours, Active Time, Productive Time, Idle Time, Attendance Status, Current Work Session, Current Project, Current Task, Weekly Trends, Monthly Trends, Quick Actions, Notifications, and AI Insights.

### 2. Timeline

Provide a complete daily activity view including Activity Timeline, Work Sessions, Application Usage, Website Usage, Idle Sessions, Breaks, Meetings, Activity Details, and Session History.

### 3. Timesheets

Allow employees and managers to view Daily, Weekly, and Monthly Timesheets, create Manual Time Entries, edit entries, submit timesheets, approve timesheets, reject timesheets, and review historical records.

### 4. Reports

Provide Work Hours Reports, Productivity Reports, Utilization Reports, Attendance Reports, Employee Reports, Team Reports, Department Reports, Overtime Reports, Trend Analysis, Exports, and Custom Report Builder.

### 5. Team Workspace (Manager)

Allow managers to monitor Team Productivity, Team Hours, Employee Activity, Team Attendance, Employee Comparisons, Team Utilization, Pending Approvals, Team Insights, and Workforce Trends.

### 6. Organization Workspace (Admin)

Allow administrators to manage Time Tracking Policies, Tracking Rules, Idle Time Rules, User Management, Team Management, Department Management, Audit Logs, Compliance Controls, Organization Analytics, and Workforce Monitoring.

### 7. Settings

Manage Tracking Preferences, Notifications, Privacy Controls, Integrations, Security Settings, Work Schedules, and Organization Policies.

Features that should be integrated across these screens include Automatic Time Tracking, Manual Time Tracking, Work Hours Tracking, Idle Time Detection, Daily Timeline, Application Tracking, Website Tracking, Attendance Integration, Productivity Analysis, Utilization Metrics, AI Insights, Notifications, Search, Filters, Approvals, Exports, and Role-Based Permissions.

Design complete navigation, breadcrumbs, dashboards, tables, analytics, charts, workflows, empty states, loading states, and responsive experiences suitable for organizations ranging from 10 to 100,000+ employees.
