Design a simple MVP Projects & Teams module for an AI Workforce Intelligence Platform. The goal is to help companies organize projects, assign work, track progress, and monitor team performance without unnecessary complexity.

### 1. Projects Dashboard

Display Total Projects, Active Projects, Completed Projects, Delayed Projects, Total Tasks, Pending Tasks, Completed Tasks, Team Members, and Project Progress Overview.

### 2. Projects

Allow users to create, edit, archive, and manage projects. Show project name, owner, team members, status, start date, due date, completion percentage, and project progress.

### 3. Project Details

Display project overview, assigned team members, tasks, milestones, recent activities, deadlines, and project progress.

### 4. Tasks

Allow users to create, assign, update, and complete tasks. Include assignee, priority, due date, status, comments, and task progress.

### 5. Teams

Manage teams and team members. Show team size, active projects, assigned tasks, and team performance summary.

### 6. Departments

Manage departments and department members. Display department overview, projects, and productivity metrics.

### 7. Milestones

Create and track project milestones. Show milestone status, due dates, owners, and completion progress.

### 8. Goals

Create simple project goals and track achievement progress.

### 9. Reports

Generate Project Reports, Task Reports, Team Reports, and Progress Reports.

### 10. Manager View

Allow managers to monitor projects, team workload, pending tasks, delayed tasks, milestone progress, and team activity.

### 11. Admin View

Allow administrators to manage projects, teams, departments, permissions, and project settings.

### 12. Settings

Manage project statuses, task priorities, permissions, notifications, and workflows.

Include dashboards, tables, kanban boards, task lists, project timelines, filters, search, notifications, role-based permissions, and basic analytics. Focus on simplicity, ease of use, and fast execution for small and medium-sized teams.
