Breadcrumb:

Dashboard > Productivity Tracking

Design a complete Productivity Tracking module for an AI Workforce Intelligence Platform. The module should help employees improve performance, managers optimize team productivity, and executives understand workforce efficiency. Create the following primary screens:

### 1. Productivity Dashboard

Display Productivity Score, Focus Time, Productive Time, Unproductive Time, Daily Productivity, Weekly Productivity, Team Productivity, Productivity Trends, Top Performers, Employees Needing Attention, Workforce Efficiency Score, and AI Insights.

### 2. Productivity Analysis

Provide detailed productivity breakdowns by employee, team, department, project, role, and location. Include productivity trends, productivity distribution, efficiency analysis, and comparison views.

### 3. Focus Time Analytics

Track deep work sessions, uninterrupted work periods, distraction analysis, focus trends, focus goals, and focus benchmarks. Show individual and team focus performance.

### 4. Productive vs Unproductive Time

Analyze productive activities versus non-productive activities. Include application analysis, website analysis, activity classification, category breakdowns, trends, and improvement opportunities.

### 5. Daily Productivity

Provide daily productivity summaries including productivity score, focus time, active time, idle time, work sessions, achievements, productivity timeline, and AI-generated insights.

### 6. Weekly Productivity

Provide weekly productivity summaries, trends, team comparisons, goal progress, productivity rankings, workload analysis, and performance highlights.

### 7. Team Productivity Workspace

Allow managers to monitor team productivity, employee comparisons, productivity rankings, workload distribution, utilization, engagement levels, and team performance insights.

### 8. Employee Productivity Details

Provide detailed employee productivity profiles showing productivity history, focus trends, work patterns, productivity drivers, risks, strengths, and recommendations.

### 9. Organization Productivity Workspace

Allow administrators and executives to monitor organization-wide productivity, department performance, workforce efficiency, productivity benchmarks, trends, and operational insights.

### 10. Productivity Reports

Provide Daily Reports, Weekly Reports, Monthly Reports, Employee Reports, Team Reports, Department Reports, Productivity Reports, Efficiency Reports, Focus Reports, Benchmark Reports, and Custom Report Builder.

### 11. AI Insights Center

Provide productivity analysis, focus analysis, work pattern analysis, productivity forecasting, burnout indicators, performance risks, optimization opportunities, and actionable recommendations.

### 12. Goals & Benchmarks

Allow organizations to define productivity goals, focus targets, team benchmarks, department benchmarks, performance targets, and progress tracking.

### 13. Settings

Manage productivity rules, activity classifications, productivity scoring logic, focus definitions, benchmarks, notifications, integrations, permissions, and organization-wide productivity policies.

Features such as Productivity Score, Focus Time, Productive vs Unproductive Time, Daily Productivity, Weekly Productivity, and Team Productivity should be integrated across these screens rather than treated as separate standalone modules. Include complete navigation, breadcrumbs, filters, search, analytics, dashboards, charts, leaderboards, comparisons, reports, AI insights, role-based permissions, notifications, empty states, loading states, and responsive layouts suitable for organizations ranging from 10 to 100,000+ employees.
