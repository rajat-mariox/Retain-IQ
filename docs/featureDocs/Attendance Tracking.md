---

Breadcrumb:

Dashboard > Attendance Tracking

Design a complete Attendance Tracking module for an AI Workforce Intelligence Platform. The module should help employees manage attendance, managers monitor team attendance, and administrators manage workforce schedules, shifts, holidays, and leave policies. Create the following primary screens:

### 1. Attendance Dashboard

Display Present Employees, Absent Employees, Late Arrivals, Employees on Leave, Attendance Rate, Team Attendance, Attendance Trends, Upcoming Holidays, Shift Status, Attendance Alerts, and AI Insights.

### 2. Check In / Check Out

Allow employees to check in and check out, view work status, attendance logs, location verification, device information, working hours, break tracking, overtime tracking, and attendance history.

### 3. Attendance Records

Provide daily, weekly, monthly, and custom attendance views. Include employee attendance history, attendance status, late arrivals, early departures, missed check-ins, attendance corrections, and attendance details.

### 4. Leave Management

Allow employees to request leave, view leave balances, track leave history, upload supporting documents, and monitor approval status. Allow managers to approve, reject, and review leave requests.

### 5. Leave Approval Center

Provide managers and administrators with pending requests, approval workflows, leave calendars, team availability views, conflict detection, and leave analytics.

### 6. Holiday Management

Manage company holidays, regional holidays, department-specific holidays, holiday calendars, upcoming holidays, and holiday policies.

### 7. Shift Management

Create and manage shifts, assign employees, define shift rules, track shift attendance, monitor shift coverage, manage rotations, and view shift analytics.

### 8. Work Schedule Management

Manage employee schedules, work weeks, flexible schedules, remote work schedules, hybrid schedules, recurring schedules, and schedule exceptions.

### 9. Team Workspace (Manager)

Allow managers to monitor team attendance, team availability, leave requests, shift coverage, attendance trends, absenteeism, punctuality metrics, and team insights.

### 10. Organization Workspace (Admin)

Allow administrators to manage attendance policies, leave policies, holiday calendars, shift rules, work schedules, compliance settings, attendance audits, departments, teams, and organization-wide workforce analytics.

### 11. Reports

Provide Attendance Reports, Leave Reports, Holiday Reports, Shift Reports, Schedule Reports, Employee Reports, Team Reports, Department Reports, Absenteeism Reports, Overtime Reports, Compliance Reports, and Custom Report Builder.

### 12. AI Insights Center

Provide attendance analysis, absenteeism trends, punctuality analysis, workforce availability insights, leave forecasting, staffing recommendations, attendance anomalies, and actionable recommendations.

### 13. Settings

Manage attendance rules, geofencing settings, work schedules, leave types, holiday settings, shift settings, notifications, integrations, security settings, and compliance controls.

Features such as Check In / Check Out, Attendance Reports, Leave Management, Holidays, Shift Management, and Work Schedule should be integrated across these screens rather than treated as separate standalone modules. Include complete navigation, breadcrumbs, filters, search, analytics, approvals, permissions, alerts, dashboards, tables, charts, empty states, loading states, and responsive layouts suitable for organizations ranging from 10 to 100,000+ employees.
