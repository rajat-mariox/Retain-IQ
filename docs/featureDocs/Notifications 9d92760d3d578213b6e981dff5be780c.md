Design a simple MVP Notifications module for an AI Workforce Intelligence Platform. The goal is to help employees, managers, administrators, and executives stay informed about important workforce events, productivity changes, attendance issues, project updates, and AI-generated recommendations.

### 1. Notifications Dashboard

Display Total Notifications, Unread Notifications, Critical Alerts, Recent Notifications, AI Alerts, and Notification Activity Summary.

### 2. Notification Center

Provide a centralized inbox for all notifications. Allow users to view, filter, search, read, archive, dismiss, and manage notifications.

### 3. Productivity Alerts

Notify users about productivity drops, productivity improvements, focus time achievements, unusual activity patterns, inactivity, and performance-related alerts.

### 4. Attendance Alerts

Notify users about missed check-ins, missed check-outs, late arrivals, absences, leave approvals, leave rejections, shift changes, and attendance anomalies.

### 5. Project Alerts

Notify users about task assignments, task completions, approaching deadlines, overdue tasks, milestone completions, project delays, project risks, and team updates.

### 6. AI Recommendations

Provide AI-generated recommendations related to productivity improvement, workload balancing, burnout prevention, employee engagement, team optimization, and workforce performance.

### 7. Email Notifications

Allow users to manage email notification preferences, delivery schedules, notification categories, email summaries, and subscription settings.

### 8. Push Notifications

Allow users to manage browser, desktop, and mobile push notifications, notification priorities, delivery rules, and quiet hours.

### 9. Manager Workspace

Allow managers to monitor team alerts, employee alerts, approval requests, project risks, attendance issues, productivity changes, and AI recommendations.

### 10. Admin Workspace

Allow administrators to manage notification rules, alert categories, delivery channels, organization-wide policies, escalation rules, and notification settings.

### 11. Notification Settings

Manage notification preferences, alert priorities, delivery methods, frequency settings, notification categories, integrations, and user-specific configurations.

Include notification feeds, alert cards, filters, search, unread indicators, priority levels, bulk actions, notification history, delivery tracking, role-based permissions, dashboards, and responsive layouts. Focus on making notifications actionable, organized, and useful without overwhelming users.
