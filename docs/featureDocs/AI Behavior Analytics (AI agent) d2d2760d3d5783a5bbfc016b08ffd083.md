Design the flagship AI Behavior Analytics module for an AI Workforce Intelligence Platform. This module serves as the intelligence layer of the entire system by transforming employee activity, attendance, productivity, project, communication, and behavioral data into actionable workforce insights. The goal is to help managers and executives understand employee performance, work habits, risks, growth potential, and organizational health through AI-powered analysis.

### 1. AI Behavior Analytics Dashboard

Display Employee Scores, Workforce Health Score, Burnout Risk Count, Attrition Risk Count, High Performers, Rising Stars, Employees Needing Attention, Team Health Score, Productivity Trends, Workforce Trends, AI Alerts, and Executive Insights.

### 2. Employee Intelligence Profile

Provide a complete AI-generated profile for every employee including Employee Score, Productivity Score, Reliability Score, Collaboration Score, Focus Score, Leadership Score, Knowledge Score, Attendance Score, Growth Potential Score, and overall Workforce Impact Score.

### 3. Work Pattern Analysis

Analyze daily routines, work habits, focus periods, meeting patterns, productivity cycles, overtime trends, work consistency, work-life balance indicators, and behavioral changes over time.

### 4. Behavioral Insights Center

Provide AI-generated behavioral observations, strengths, weaknesses, engagement levels, motivation indicators, communication patterns, collaboration trends, and performance drivers.

### 5. Burnout Detection Center

Identify employees at risk of burnout using workload, overtime, attendance, productivity changes, work patterns, and behavioral indicators. Include risk levels, contributing factors, trends, recommendations, and intervention suggestions.

### 6. Attrition Risk Center

Identify employees likely to resign using behavioral changes, engagement levels, attendance patterns, productivity shifts, tenure data, promotion history, compensation signals, and workforce trends. Include risk scores, confidence levels, key indicators, and retention recommendations.

### 7. Productivity Recommendation Engine

Generate AI-powered recommendations for employees, managers, and executives to improve productivity, focus, collaboration, utilization, workload balance, and workforce efficiency.

### 8. AI Daily Summary

Provide automated daily summaries for employees, managers, and executives. Include achievements, productivity highlights, work patterns, attendance insights, focus analysis, risks, alerts, and recommendations.

### 9. AI Weekly Summary

Provide executive-level weekly summaries covering employee performance, team performance, workforce health, productivity trends, burnout risks, attrition risks, top performers, operational risks, and strategic recommendations.

### 10. Team Intelligence Workspace

Allow managers to analyze team behavior, engagement, collaboration, productivity, utilization, burnout risks, attrition risks, team health scores, and workforce trends.

### 11. Workforce Risk Center

Centralized dashboard for workforce risks including burnout risks, attrition risks, engagement risks, performance risks, attendance risks, and organizational health indicators.

### 12. Executive AI Copilot

Allow executives to ask questions such as:

- Who are my best employees?
- Who is likely to resign?
- Who deserves promotion?
- Which teams are at risk?
- Who is showing burnout signals?
- Which manager performs best?
- Where is productivity declining?
- Which employees create the most business value?

### 13. AI Recommendations Center

Provide promotion recommendations, retention recommendations, coaching opportunities, learning recommendations, workload balancing suggestions, succession planning insights, and workforce optimization opportunities.

### 14. Workforce Intelligence Reports

Generate Employee Intelligence Reports, Team Intelligence Reports, Burnout Reports, Attrition Reports, Workforce Health Reports, Leadership Reports, Productivity Reports, Risk Reports, and Executive Reports.

### 15. AI Model & Scoring Settings

Manage scoring models, risk thresholds, prediction settings, recommendation logic, confidence levels, AI governance, explainability, permissions, and organization-wide AI policies.
